A homelab zine by Siem Houkes

I'm Siem Houkes, in Tilburg, NL. I build small tools that run around the clock on a Raspberry Pi 5 at home. Most are just for me and stay behind a login; the open ones are linked, and some of the code is on GitHub.

Sunroute

A road-trip weather planner: compare a string of stops on one map across the next ten days and see where the sun will be. Made for fast route decisions, not deep forecasting.

Hut Watcher

Polls alpine hut booking pages for freed-up beds and highlights exactly what changed on a live dashboard, built for catching last-minute cancellations on hut-to-hut routes.

NO.04

PeakView

Point the phone at the mountains and see peak names, heights and distances on the camera, with terrain occlusion, so a label only lands on a summit you can actually see.

NO.05

Vogelspoor

Geotags bird photos from the GPX track of a watch or phone, entirely in the browser: nothing gets uploaded. Fixes wrong camera clocks and writes GPS straight into the JPEG, ready for waarneming.nl and eBird.

  • GPX
  • EXIF
  • client-side

Last-minute student theatre deals around Tilburg. Rather than checking what's cheap right now, it tracks how fast each hall fills and forecasts which shows will still have seats when the discount window opens.
